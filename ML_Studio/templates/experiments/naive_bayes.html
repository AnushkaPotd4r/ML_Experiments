Bayes' Theorem Formula

The posterior probability is proportional to prior times Gaussian likelihood:

P(Ck | X) ∝ P(Ck) ∏ P(xi | Ck)

Gaussian likelihood assumes normal distribution: N(x; μik, σik²).
